PORTABLE AI - START HERE
=======================

1. Double-click  Start-AI.cmd
2. Wait for your browser to open (http://127.0.0.1:8099).
3. Ask a question, or open "Binary Lab" to analyze an .exe/.dll.

Stop it with  Stop-AI.cmd  (or close the console window).

Works offline. Nothing is installed on this PC.
First start may take 10-30 seconds from a USB drive.

Phone/tablet on the same Wi-Fi? Use Start-AI-LAN.cmd and open the
token URL shown in the console or in Settings -> LAN access.

Check the install:  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\verify-install.ps1

Docs: README.md and the docs folder.
Safety: analyze only files you own or are authorized to inspect.
This tool does not help bypass exams, proctoring, DRM or access controls.
